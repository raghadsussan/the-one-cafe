# Sources and assets

## Original assets

The café scene in assets/central-perk-vintage.png was created specifically for this redesign using OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. It is an AI-generated interpretation of a 1990s Central Perk interior and its iconic orange sofa, not a frame from the series or a photograph of the original prop. The supplied reference photos informed the requested atmosphere; they are not bundled. The generated scene includes Central Perk signage.

Generation brief: photorealistic empty 1990s Central Perk-inspired café; iconic burnt-orange velvet sofa with carved wood and rolled arms; exposed brick and muted plum walls; antique brass lamps, Persian rug, mullioned window; cup and notebook on foreground wooden table, vintage radio to the right; warm tungsten light, analog film texture, no people or UI.

The image is already bundled. Keep the assets folder beside index.html when publishing. No external fonts, audio or image downloads are needed.

All ambience is generated by app.js using Web Audio noise buffers and filters. No third-party recordings or Friends music are used.

Friends and Central Perk belong to their respective rights holders. This is an unofficial fan-inspired project with no affiliation or endorsement. Generated artwork is not official licensed series artwork.

## Personal café additions

Weather, the interactive mug illustration and typography styling are original HTML/CSS/SVG. The mug includes fan-themed Central Perk lettering. The twelve rotating jokes are newly written fan-inspired lines, not quotations attributed to the series; the first uses the user's supplied wording. No official show audio, soundtrack or actor voice recordings are bundled. Six ambience options are generated in app.js, including a short original arpeggiated string texture. The optional audio picker uses a local object URL only, does not transmit the file, and does not include it in the project ZIP. Use files you have permission to use.

## Edition 04 image variants

assets/weather-rain.png, assets/weather-snow.png and assets/weather-fog.png were created with the built-in image-generation tool by editing the project's original scene. The edit brief requested the same framing, furniture, sofa, table, cup, notebook and radio while changing the view through the left window to realistic rainy New York, settled snow/falling outdoor snow, or misty buildings. These are AI-created still images, not original series footage and not a live weather feed. All four scene images are included in this ZIP.
